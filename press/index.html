01Biographies

Three lengths, all approved.

Short — 45 words

Irini Pericleous is a Cypriot violinist and vocalist. She played as a soloist with the “Oltenia” Philharmonic of Craiova, studied in masterclasses with Salvatore Accardo and Alexandru Tomescu, and reached the final four of The Voice of Greece in 2021, at seventeen — singing and playing at the same time. She is the live violin and voice in Vincent Simone’s Tango Passions, which she has toured the United Kingdom with since 2022.

Medium — 110 words

Irini Pericleous is a violinist and vocalist from Nicosia, Cyprus. She began the violin at seven, joined the Cyprus Youth Symphony Orchestra in 2014, and was invited to Craiova in 2015 as a soloist with the “Oltenia” Philharmonic. She has studied in masterclasses with Salvatore Accardo and Alexandru Tomescu, and played the Musikverein in Vienna.

She has sung on Greek national television since 2016. At her blind audition for The Voice of Greece she played the violin and sang at once, and at seventeen was one of the last four in the final. Her first single, “Βιάστηκα Πολύ”, was released by Heaven Music in 2022. Since that year she has toured the United Kingdom as the live violin and voice in Vincent Simone’s Tango Passions.

Long — 280 words

Irini Pericleous is a violinist and vocalist from Nicosia, Cyprus.

She started the violin at seven, taught by Sorin Alexandru Horlea of the Cyprus Symphony Orchestra, and gave her first recital at eleven at the Kasteliotissa Medieval Hall in Nicosia. She joined the Cyprus Youth Symphony Orchestra in 2014 under George Koundouri, playing with it in programmes alongside the pianist Cyprien Katsaris and in productions including Cavalleria Rusticana. In November 2015 she was invited to Romania as a soloist with the “Oltenia” Philharmonic of Craiova — her first concert outside Cyprus.

She has studied in masterclasses with Salvatore Accardo, in Cyprus in 2016, and with Alexandru Tomescu in Romania, closing that week with a concert in the music hall of Bran Castle. In 2016 she passed Grade 8 violin with the Royal Schools of Music at the highest mark. She was selected from a field of candidates to travel to Vienna and play at the Musikverein with the Cyprus Youth Symphony Orchestra. In May 2018 she gave a full solo recital in Nicosia, “For My Mother”, with a programme running from Bach and Vivaldi to Kreisler, Tchaikovsky and Porumbescu’s Balada.

She has sung on Greek national television since 2016. She was one of eight finalists on Junior Music Stars; auditioned for X Factor Greece in 2019 with “Feeling Good”, in a broadcast since watched close to a million and a half times; and in October 2020 auditioned for The Voice of Greece, playing the violin and singing “I Put a Spell on You” at the same time. Chosen by Eleonora Zouganeli and taken by Sakis Rouvas at the knockouts, she was one of the four left contesting the title in the final on 12 February 2021, at seventeen, and the only one of the four from his team.

Her first single, “Βιάστηκα Πολύ” — written and composed by Thomas Theodorou — was released by Heaven Music in May 2022, with a video directed by Lefteris Giannopoulos in which she both sings and plays.

She went on to study musical theatre in England, and has toured the United Kingdom as the live violin and voice in Tango Passions, Vincent Simone’s production for Strictly Theatre Co, since its opening night in September 2022. In a show whose music is otherwise recorded, she plays it: the Fine Times Recorder noted that apart from her the score was “well recorded and produced canned”. Reviewing the opening night, Grapevine Magazine gave the show five stars and wrote that the recorded music was “brought alive by the exceptional talent of the live violinist who played with intensity and a heart-wrenchingly exquisite tone”. The Shropshire Star credited its “impassioned Spanish language songs and violin music” to “the multi-talented Irini Pericleous”, and in October 2025 My Shrewsbury described her “atmospheric, and at times melancholic, accompaniment”. In Cyprus she has been billed on her own stage at the MoonWalk festival in Protaras in two consecutive editions. She performs in English, Greek and Spanish.

02Written about

In print.

In Tango Passions she is the only live musician on stage — the rest of the score is recorded. Some reviewers print her name; others write about “the live violinist”. Both are her.

03Fact sheet

The facts, dated.

2015

Soloist — “Oltenia” Philharmonic, Craiova

Invited to Romania as a soloist with the Oltenia Philharmonic Orchestra under its Europa Season programme. Her first concert outside Cyprus.

2016

Masterclasses — Salvatore Accardo, Alexandru Tomescu

Studies with Salvatore Accardo in Cyprus, and with Alexandru Tomescu in Romania, closing that course with a concert in the music hall of Bran Castle. Passes Grade 8 violin with the Royal Schools of Music at the highest mark.

2017

Junior Music Stars — finalist

One of eight finalists on the SKAI series, playing Monti's Czárdás in the semi-final and opening the final with “You Raise Me Up” on violin.

2018

“For My Mother” — solo recital, Nicosia

A full solo programme at the Kasteliotissa Medieval Hall: Bach, Vivaldi, Gluck, Kreisler, Pugnani, Chopin, Tchaikovsky, John Williams, and Porumbescu's Balada.

2019

X Factor Greece

Auditions with “Feeling Good”. The broadcast has since been watched more than a million times, and reaches the Chair Challenge.

2021

The Voice of Greece — the final four

At seventeen, three of the four coaches turn at her blind audition, playing and singing “I Put a Spell on You” at once. She picks Eleonora Zouganeli, wins her knockout, is taken by Sakis Rouvas, and in the final on 12 February 2021 is one of the four left contesting the title — the only one of them from Sakis Rouvas’s team.

2022

“Βιάστηκα Πολύ” — Heaven Music

Her first single, written and composed by Thomas Theodorou, with a video directed by Lefteris Giannopoulos. The label introduced her as the newest addition to its roster.

2022

Tango Passions — UK tours

The live violin and voice in Vincent Simone's touring production for Strictly Theatre Co — in a show whose music is otherwise recorded, she plays it. She has been in it since its opening night at the Spa Pavilion, Felixstowe, and reviewers have written about her at six venues between 2022 and 2025, from Weston-super-Mare to the Grand Opera House in Belfast.
