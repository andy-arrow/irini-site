Biographies

Three lengths, all approved. And one in Greek.

Short — 60 words

Irini Pericleous is a violinist who sings while she plays. Cypriot, based in the United Kingdom, she reached the final four of The Voice of Greece in 2021, at seventeen. She is the only live musician in Vincent Simone’s Tango Passions — violin and voice over a recorded score — and played violin on Anton Du Beke’s 2023 UK tour.

Medium — 110 words

Irini Pericleous is a violinist and vocalist from Cyprus, based in the United Kingdom and working worldwide. She began the violin at seven, joined the Cyprus Youth Symphony Orchestra in 2014, and was invited to Craiova in 2015 as a soloist with the “Oltenia” Philharmonic. She has studied in masterclasses with Salvatore Accardo and Alexandru Tomescu, and in 2025 was selected for the Festival d’Aix-en-Provence’s Mediterranean Youth Orchestra, which Cyprus enters through its youth symphony orchestra.

Greek television introduced her: Junior Music Stars, X Factor and, in 2021, The Voice of Greece, where three of the four coaches turned at a blind audition she played and sang at the same time, and where she was one of the last four in the final. Since 2022 she has been the live violin and voice in Vincent Simone’s Tango Passions, and in 2023 she held the violin chair on Anton Du Beke’s UK tour. She also performs live over DJ sets, in techno, house and afro-house.

Long — 280 words

Irini Pericleous is a violinist and vocalist from Cyprus. She is based in the United Kingdom and available worldwide.

She started the violin at seven, taught by Sorin Alexandru Horlea of the Cyprus Symphony Orchestra, and gave her first recital at eleven at the Kasteliotissa Medieval Hall in Nicosia. She joined the Cyprus Youth Symphony Orchestra in 2014 under George Koundouri and has played with it since — in programmes alongside Alkinoos Ioannidis and Alkistis Protopsalti, with the pianist Cyprien Katsaris, and, chosen from the orchestra, in its annual Cyprus Concert at the Musikverein in Vienna. In November 2015 she was invited to Romania as a soloist with the “Oltenia” Philharmonic of Craiova — her first concert outside Cyprus.

She has studied in masterclasses with Salvatore Accardo, in Cyprus in 2016, and with Alexandru Tomescu in Romania, closing that week with a concert in the music hall of Bran Castle. In 2016 she passed Grade 8 violin with the Royal Schools of Music at the highest mark. In May 2018 she gave a full solo recital in Nicosia, “For My Mother”, with a programme running from Bach and Vivaldi to Kreisler, Tchaikovsky and Porumbescu’s Balada. In 2025 she was selected for the Orchestre des Jeunes de la Méditerranée, the Festival d’Aix-en-Provence’s Mediterranean Youth Orchestra — ninety-one players from twenty-two nationalities, coached by tutors from the London Symphony Orchestra — which Cyprus enters through the Cyprus Youth Symphony Orchestra.

Greek national television is where most people first saw her. She was one of eight finalists on Junior Music Stars; she auditioned for X Factor Greece in 2019 with “Feeling Good”, in a broadcast since watched close to a million and a half times; and in 2020 she auditioned for The Voice of Greece, playing the violin and singing “I Put a Spell on You” at the same time. Three of the four coaches turned. Chosen by Eleonora Zouganeli and taken by Sakis Rouvas at the knockouts, she was one of the four left contesting the title in the final on 12 February 2021, at seventeen, and the only one of the four from his team.

She went on to study musical theatre in England. Since its opening night in September 2022 she has toured the United Kingdom as the live violin and voice in Tango Passions, Vincent Simone’s production for Strictly Theatre Co: in a show whose music is otherwise recorded, she plays it, and the Fine Times Recorder noted that apart from her the score was “well recorded and produced canned”. Reviewing the opening night, Grapevine Magazine gave the show five stars and wrote that the recorded music was “brought alive by the exceptional talent of the live violinist who played with intensity and a heart-wrenchingly exquisite tone”. The Shropshire Star credited its “impassioned Spanish language songs and violin music” to “the multi-talented Irini Pericleous”, and in October 2025 My Shrewsbury described her “atmospheric, and at times melancholic, accompaniment”. The tour has reached the Bloomsbury Theatre in London.

In 2023 she held the violin chair in Anton Du Beke’s band for An Evening with Anton Du Beke and Friends, under the musical director Clive Dunstall and alongside the vocalist Lance Ellington; the tour played theatres across the country, the London Palladium among them. Reviewing it, Seen and Heard International wrote that the very best dance routine was “a dramatic tango featuring Irini Pericleous’s fiery Spanish violin”.

Alongside the concert and theatre work she performs live over DJ sets — violin and voice written into techno, house and afro-house rather than laid on top of them — and at corporate and private engagements. She performs in English, Greek and Spanish.

Written about

In print.

In Tango Passions she is the only live musician on stage — the rest of the score is recorded. Some reviewers print her name; others write about “the live violinist”. Both are her.

Fact sheet

The facts, dated.

2015

Soloist — “Oltenia” Philharmonic, Craiova

Invited to Romania as a soloist with the Oltenia Philharmonic Orchestra under its Europa Season programme. Her first concert outside Cyprus.

2016

Masterclasses — Salvatore Accardo, Alexandru Tomescu

Studies with Salvatore Accardo in Cyprus, and with Alexandru Tomescu in Romania, closing that course with a concert in the music hall of Bran Castle. Passes Grade 8 violin with the Royal Schools of Music at the highest mark.

2017

Junior Music Stars — finalist

One of eight finalists on the SKAI series, playing Monti's Czárdás in the semi-final and opening the final with “You Raise Me Up” on violin.

2018

“For My Mother” — solo recital, Nicosia

A full solo programme at the Kasteliotissa Medieval Hall: Bach, Vivaldi, Gluck, Kreisler, Pugnani, Chopin, Tchaikovsky, John Williams, and Porumbescu's Balada.

2019

X Factor Greece

Auditions with “Feeling Good”. The broadcast has since been watched more than a million times, and reaches the Chair Challenge.

2021

The Voice of Greece — the final four

At seventeen, three of the four coaches turn at her blind audition, playing and singing “I Put a Spell on You” at once. She picks Eleonora Zouganeli, wins her knockout, is taken by Sakis Rouvas, and in the final on 12 February 2021 is one of the four left contesting the title — the only one of them from Sakis Rouvas’s team.

2022

Tango Passions — UK tours

The live violin and voice in Vincent Simone's touring production for Strictly Theatre Co — in a show whose music is otherwise recorded, she plays it. She has been in it since its opening night at the Spa Pavilion, Felixstowe, and reviewers have written about her at six venues between 2022 and 2025, from Weston-super-Mare to the Grand Opera House in Belfast.

2023

An Evening with Anton Du Beke and Friends — UK tour

The violin chair in Anton Du Beke's band for the 2023 tour, under musical director Clive Dunstall and alongside the guest vocalist Lance Ellington. Seen and Heard International named her in its band list and called the show's best routine “a dramatic tango featuring Irini Pericleous's fiery Spanish violin”. The tour played the London Palladium on 1 October 2023, billed there as An Evening with Anton Du Beke.

2025

Orchestre des Jeunes de la Méditerranée

Selected for the Mediterranean Youth Orchestra of the Festival d'Aix-en-Provence — ninety-one players aged eighteen to twenty-six drawn from twenty-two nationalities, coached by tutors from the London Symphony Orchestra under Evan Rogister. Cyprus enters the orchestra through the Cyprus Youth Symphony Orchestra, and she was among the musicians who carried the country into the 2025 symphonic session.
