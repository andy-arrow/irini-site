Partnerships

A musician,
not a placement.

Some briefs need a track. Others need someone to play one — on the day, in front of a camera or a room. This page is for the second kind.

01Why her

The craft is real, and it is on the record.

Most partnerships with musicians buy an association. This one buys a capability. She has played as a soloist with the “Oltenia” Philharmonic of Craiova and studied in a masterclass with Salvatore Accardo. She reached the final four of The Voice of Greece, from a blind audition she played and sang at once. Since its opening night in 2022 she has been the only live musician in Vincent Simone’s Tango Passions, playing live where the rest of the score is recorded.

That combination travels. It is credible to a cultural institution and legible to a mass audience.

She performs in English, Greek, Spanish, is based in the United Kingdom, and works worldwide.

02Formats

Ways of working.

Campaign

Stills and film. She has performed on national television since she was a teenager and has toured the United Kingdom since 2022 as the only live musician in a touring production — so cameras, a schedule and a live take are familiar conditions rather than new ones.

Live moment

A performance at a launch, a dinner, a store opening or a private event — solo, or with musicians engaged for the occasion. Repertoire is built to the room rather than pulled from a set list.

Original music

A violin line written for a film, an identity or a campaign. Recorded and delivered, with or without vocal.

Content and ambassadorship

Longer-form association where the work is genuinely hers. Her audience arrived for performances, and responds to performance.
