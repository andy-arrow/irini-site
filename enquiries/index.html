What she is asked for

DJ sets & club nights
Live violin and vocals over techno, house and afro-house. A ten-minute feature or a whole night, built to the set you send her.
Concerts & festivals
Solo, or with musicians engaged for the programme. Classical, crossover and popular repertoire, in English, Greek and Spanish.
Corporate & private
Openings, dinners, ceremonies and celebrations, where the performance is the point rather than the background.
Brands & campaigns
Campaign work, live moments at brand events, original music and longer associations.
Press & collaboration
Interviews, features and photography; writers, producers and artists proposing work together.
