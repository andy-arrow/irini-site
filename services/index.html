04

Concert & orchestral

Classical training that goes back to the age of seven, and a place in the Cyprus Youth Symphony Orchestra since 2014. She has appeared as a soloist with the “Oltenia” Philharmonic of Craiova and studied in masterclasses with Salvatore Accardo and Alexandru Tomescu.

  • —Solo recital
  • —Soloist with orchestra
  • —Classical, crossover and popular repertoire
  • —In English, Greek and Spanish

In 2025 she was selected for the Festival d’Aix-en-Provence’s Mediterranean Youth Orchestra, which Cyprus enters through its youth symphony orchestra.
