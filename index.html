01The short version

Most performers choose. She never did.

A classical violinist since the age of seven and a singer on national television since 2016 — often at the same moment. The two halves are not a novelty act. They are two disciplines carried to a serious level by one performer.

The violin

Grade 8 with the Royal Schools of Music at the highest mark, in 2016.

Soloist with the “Oltenia” Philharmonic of Craiova. Masterclasses with Salvatore Accardo and Alexandru Tomescu, the second closing with a concert at Bran Castle.

A member of the Cyprus Youth Symphony Orchestra since 2014.

The voice

The final four of The Voice of Greece, from a blind audition she played and sang at the same time.

An X Factor Greece audition watched close to a million and a half times on the broadcaster's own channel.

Lead violinist and lead vocalist in touring theatre, playing live where the rest of the score is recorded.

The audience

54K on TikTok and 1.6M likes, from covers and performance.

Her performances have been watched more than four and a half million times across official channels.

She performs in English, Greek and Spanish.

03Chronology

A record, not a résumé.

  1. 2015

    Soloist — “Oltenia” Philharmonic, Craiova

    Invited to Romania as a soloist with the Oltenia Philharmonic Orchestra under its Europa Season programme. Her first concert outside Cyprus.

  2. 2016

    Masterclasses — Salvatore Accardo, Alexandru Tomescu

    Studies with Salvatore Accardo in Cyprus, and with Alexandru Tomescu in Romania, closing that course with a concert in the music hall of Bran Castle. Passes Grade 8 violin with the Royal Schools of Music at the highest mark.

  3. 2017

    Junior Music Stars — finalist

    One of eight finalists on the SKAI series, playing Monti's Czárdás in the semi-final and opening the final with “You Raise Me Up” on violin.

  4. 2018

    “For My Mother” — solo recital, Nicosia

    A full solo programme at the Kasteliotissa Medieval Hall: Bach, Vivaldi, Gluck, Kreisler, Pugnani, Chopin, Tchaikovsky, John Williams, and Porumbescu's Balada.

  5. 2019

    X Factor Greece

    Auditions with “Feeling Good”. The broadcast has since been watched more than a million times, and reaches the Chair Challenge.

  6. 2021

    The Voice of Greece — the final four

    At seventeen, three of the four coaches turn at her blind audition, playing and singing “I Put a Spell on You” at once. She picks Eleonora Zouganeli, wins her knockout, is taken by Sakis Rouvas, and in the final on 12 February 2021 is one of the four left contesting the title — the only one of them from Sakis Rouvas’s team.

  7. 2022

    Tango Passions — UK tours

    The live violin and voice in Vincent Simone's touring production for Strictly Theatre Co — in a show whose music is otherwise recorded, she plays it. She has been in it since its opening night at the Spa Pavilion, Felixstowe, and reviewers have written about her at six venues between 2022 and 2025, from Weston-super-Mare to the Grand Opera House in Belfast.

  8. 2023

    An Evening with Anton Du Beke and Friends — UK tour

    The violin chair in Anton Du Beke's band for the 2023 tour, under musical director Clive Dunstall and alongside the guest vocalist Lance Ellington. Seen and Heard International named her in its band list and called the show's best routine “a dramatic tango featuring Irini Pericleous's fiery Spanish violin”. The tour played the London Palladium on 1 October 2023, billed there as An Evening with Anton Du Beke.

  9. 2025

    Orchestre des Jeunes de la Méditerranée

    Selected for the Mediterranean Youth Orchestra of the Festival d'Aix-en-Provence — ninety-one players aged eighteen to twenty-six drawn from twenty-two nationalities, coached by tutors from the London Symphony Orchestra under Evan Rogister. Cyprus enters the orchestra through the Cyprus Youth Symphony Orchestra, and she was among the musicians who carried the country into the 2025 symphonic session.

04What she does

One performer, four rooms.

The instrument and the voice stay the same. Everything around them — repertoire, arrangement, length, whether there is a DJ behind her — is built for the room she is playing.

  • With a DJ

    Live violin and vocals over techno, house and afro-house — written into the set rather than laid on top of it. Ten minutes or a whole night.

  • Theatre & shows

    Lead violinist and lead vocalist in touring productions, playing live where the rest of the score is recorded.

  • Corporate & private

    Openings, dinners, ceremonies and celebrations — the performance as the reason people look up, not as background.

  • Concert & orchestral

    Classical training that goes back to the age of seven, and a place in the Cyprus Youth Symphony Orchestra since 2014.
