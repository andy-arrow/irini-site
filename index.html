The short version

She played and sang at once. Three of the four coaches turned.

A classical violinist since the age of seven and a singer on national television since 2016 — often at the same moment. Since its opening night in 2022 she has been the only live musician in Vincent Simone’s Tango Passions, playing live where the rest of the score is recorded.

The violin
Soloist with the “Oltenia” Philharmonic of Craiova; the Cyprus Youth Symphony Orchestra since 2014.
The voice
The final four of The Voice of Greece, from a blind audition she played and sang at the same time.
The audience
54K on TikTok, and more than 4.6M views across official channels.
