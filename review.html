What we left out on purpose

Eurovision. You submitted a song to ERT for the Greek national final. Submitting is not the same as being selected, and a promoter who checks will notice — so it is not on the site.

Streaming numbers. Your Spotify listener count is low, and putting it next to 4.7 million video views would only draw attention to it. The site leads with television and video instead.

Your age. No birth year anywhere. It dates a site and it is nobody’s business.

Your family. Nothing, beyond the title of the 2018 recital.

“West End” for the Bloomsbury Theatre. The Palladium is a West End theatre; the Bloomsbury is not — it is Off-West-End, and four separate listings bodies classify it that way. A producer reading the site would know. So the site names the theatres and lets them speak, rather than using the phrase and being wrong about half of it.

“Lead violinist” and “singer” on the Du Beke tour. The review that names you credits you as “violin” and credits Lance Ellington as the vocalist. You may well have done more — if so, say so and we will put it back.
